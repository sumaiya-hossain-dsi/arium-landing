## NOTES ON SPECIFIC ITEMS

### Modal / popup for community section
The idea is viable — clicking "Community & Volunteering" opens a modal with the full list rather than a long inline section.
**Pros:** Clean, doesn't clutter the main layout.
**Cons:** Content is hidden from screen readers by default; harder for scanners; not ideal for accessibility or SEO (content not crawlable easily).
**Alternatives to consider:**
- Collapsible accordion within the Experience page
- Dedicated section on About page with clear visual weight
- A compact "Community" card on the index that expands inline

### Stack Overflow integration
Beyond just a link, a few options:
- **Link + stats in hero/social row:** Just the icon + "4,790 rep" as a text badge → clean, credible
- **Inline callout card:** A small card in the Community or Skills section: "444 contributions on Stack Overflow · 4,790 rep" with link → ties technical community engagement to skills
- **Full SO section:** Probably overkill unless blog/writing is absent and this fills that space

My lean: stats callout card somewhere in the middle of the page, plus icon in the hero social row.
